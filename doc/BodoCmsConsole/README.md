BodoCmsConsole app
============================

# What does the app
 

BodoCms is a content management system creating static webpages. It is the intention of BodoCms to avoid dynamically created webpages in contrast to common CMS like WordPress or Drupal.

Use BodoCmsConsole together with BodoFtpTransfer to update the webpages on the webserver on a scheduled base. BodoCmsConsole creates the websites locally and BodoFtpTransfer transfers it to the webserver on demand.
